Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
App: Parenthood (iOS and Android)
Contact: hatricyadav@gmail.com

We built Parenthood for parents and caregivers who are exhausted and busy. You are trusting us with details about your family, so we keep this simple: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.

The short version

  • You talk, we turn your words into baby care entries. We don't keep your voice recordings.
  • Your family's logs are visible only to the caregivers you invite.
  • No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. No selling or renting of data.
  • You can export everything or delete your account at any time, right in the app.
  • Parenthood is a journal, not a medical service.

Who we are

Parenthood is operated by Raju Yadav, an independent developer ("Parenthood", "we", "us"). For data protection law purposes, we are the controller of the personal data described here.

What we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your name (if you share it), your email address (or an Apple private relay address), and an account identifier. We never see your Apple or Google password.

Family and baby information. The name you give your family space, your baby's name, birth date and (optionally) sex, your preferred units, and the names of caregivers you invite.

Baby care logs. What you record: feeds, bottles, pumping, diapers, sleep, tummy time, medicine, temperature and notes, with their times and amounts, who logged each entry, and whether it was logged by voice or by tap. For voice entries we store the text transcript so you can see what was understood and fix mistakes.

Pregnancy and your own health logs. If you use the pregnancy and mom features: your due date, height and pre-pregnancy weight (optional), and what you record about yourself — meals and estimated nutrition, water, vitamins, weight, blood pressure, kick counts, contractions, mood, symptoms, postpartum bleeding, workouts and appointments.

Meal photos. Only when you choose to snap or pick a meal photo. The photo is resized, stripped of location and other metadata, and sent to our AI provider to estimate what's on the plate. If you keep it, it is stored privately with your family's data; otherwise it is not kept.

Assistant chat. Messages you send to the in-app assistant, its replies, and short facts you ask it to remember ("our pediatrician is Dr. Lee"). You can see and delete remembered facts in the app. Spoken replies are generated from the reply text and are not stored.

Reminders. Reminders you set ("remind me to take my prenatal at 9pm") are scheduled as notifications on your phone only.

Voice audio. When you tap the microphone, audio is streamed securely to our speech-to-text provider, which returns text in real time. Audio is never recorded or saved — not on your phone and not on our servers. Voice logging needs an internet connection; when you're offline you can type or tap to log instead. The microphone is only on after you tap it — Parenthood never listens in the background.

Technical information. Like any online service, our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address, request time and error details) to keep the service secure and working.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing history, and we never access your photo library except for a photo you pick.

How we use it

  • To provide Parenthood: create entries from your words, show your timeline, run timers, answer your questions ("when did she last eat?"), and sync between your family's devices.
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent abuse.
  • To support you when you contact us.
  • To improve accuracy only using aggregated, de-identified error information, or examples you explicitly choose to share with us. We do not use your logs or transcripts to train AI models.

Legal bases (EEA/UK users): performing our contract with you (running the app you signed up for); your consent (microphone access, which you can withdraw in your phone's settings); and our legitimate interests (security and service reliability).

We do not sell personal information and do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.

Children's privacy

Parenthood is meant for adults (18+), parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 13 to create an account. Information about a baby is entered and controlled by the parent or caregiver who uses the app. A parent can review, export, correct or delete this information at any time in the app, or by emailing us. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Health information

Your logs can include health information about your baby (such as temperature or medicine) and about you (such as pregnancy, weight, blood pressure, mood or postpartum recovery). We treat it as sensitive: we use it only to provide the app, never for advertising, and never share it except as described above. Where laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act apply, we collect it with your consent (given when you create logs) and you can withdraw consent by deleting the data or your account.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete an entry, it is removed from the app right away and erased from our systems within 30 days. When you delete your account, your account and personal data are deleted within 30 days, including from backups. If you are the only member of a family, that family's logs are deleted too. If other caregivers remain, the family's shared logs stay with them, but your account details are removed.

Your rights and choices

Everyone, wherever you live, can:

  • Export your family's data (Profile → Privacy & data → Export my data).
  • Correct any entry by editing it.
  • Delete entries, or your whole account (Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account).
  • Turn off the microphone in your phone's settings. Tap logging still works.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may also have the right to access, port, restrict or object to processing, and to know what we collect. Email hatricyadav@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days (45 days for California requests). We will never treat you differently for exercising your rights. EEA/UK residents may also complain to their local data protection authority.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Database access rules ensure that only members of a family can read that family's data. Speech-to-text keys are short-lived and single-use. No system is perfect: if a breach ever affects you, we will tell you promptly as the law requires.

International transfers

Our servers are in the United States. If you use Parenthood from elsewhere, your data is transferred to the U.S. under safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes

If we make material changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect. The effective date above shows when this policy last changed.

Medical Disclaimer

Parenthood helps you record your baby's care. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

  • Parenthood only records what you say or tap. It never recommends, adjusts or calculates medicine doses. Always follow the dosing instructions from your pediatrician or pharmacist.
  • Summaries and answers ("when did she last eat?") come from your own logs. They are only as accurate as those entries. Speech recognition and AI can make mistakes, so please check each confirmation.
  • Parenthood does not monitor your baby and cannot alert you to emergencies.
  • If you are worried about your baby's health, contact your pediatrician. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the U.S.) right away.
